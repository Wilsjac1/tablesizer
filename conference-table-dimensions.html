Conference table dimensions

Most conference tables are 29–30″ high, 36–60″ wide and 6–24 ft long. Width grows with length so people at the far end can still see and reach the center.

Height

Standard seated height is 29–30″, paired with standard office chairs. Standing-height tables run about 40–42″.

Round tables

A 48″ round seats 4 comfortably, 72″ seats 6, 96″ seats 8 and 120″ seats 10 at 36″ per person (Paul Downs seating guide).

How the numbers work

Allow 30″ of edge per person (36″ to be comfortable), one person at each end, and 42–48″ from the table edge to the wall (OFW sizing guide; OfficeSimpli). See how we size tables.
