Boardroom table sizes

Boardrooms usually use comfortable or generous spacing (36–42″ per person), which makes the table longer than a standard meeting table for the same headcount.

Shapes

  • Rectangle: most seats per foot.
  • Boat: wider in the middle so everyone can see everyone.
  • Racetrack: rounded ends, a softer look.
  • Two-piece: long tables can be built in sections for elevators and tight stairs.

Materials

Solid hardwood (walnut, white oak, maple, cherry) lasts for decades and can be refinished. Veneer and laminate cost less up front.

Questions

Conference table sizing, answered

How much space per person at a conference table?

Allow 30 inches of table edge per person as a minimum, 36 inches for comfort, and 42 inches where laptops and papers are spread out (Paul Downs; OFW).

How much clearance behind chairs?

Plan 42–48 inches from the table edge to the wall so chairs can be drawn back and people can pass behind them. 36 inches is the accessible-route minimum.

Round or rectangle conference table?

Rectangles seat the most people per foot. Round tables seat their circumference divided by the space per person, and suit smaller, equal-sided conversations.

What size table seats 10 people?

At 30 inches per person a 10-seat rectangle is about 10 feet long; at 36 inches per person, about 12 feet.

Which shape for video calls?

A U or V shape leaves a wall open for the display and seats people on the outside edge only.
